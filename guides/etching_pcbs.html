Etching PCBs

This was a lot more common in days-gone-by, when one couldn't order a PCB (at least for cheap). Nowadays, it's a lot cheaper to order one, but a big cost is time. I think etching is a good skill to have, so you can test your boards before you order a whole batch --- you can make an etched board the same day you design the circuit, and itterate a lot quicker. It's also a good way to make a ``one-off'' board to convert an SMD component to through hole or something like that, when it's not worth it to order the PCB. Plus, it can be just-plain fun!
